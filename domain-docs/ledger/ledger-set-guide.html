01

Typhen

6 objects

Six objects, six functions. Nothing carried without a reason.

Graphite ledger
Tracks outcomes, not people. Every page maps what happens when he steps into a situation.
Tri-key clasp
Opens nothing alone. Built to combine with keys held by others — he makes people choose to open doors.
Signal baton
Carries no markings. Doesn’t command — synchronizes chaos into coordinated motion.
Relay node
Amplifies whoever stands near him. His power is never centered on himself.
Matte coin
No face, no value. Not currency — a reminder that he trades in commitments.
Industrial cloth
Binds, reinforces, or repairs — whatever the moment demands.

He aligns, coordinates, reinforces, and redirects. Not a mystic, not a brute, not a wanderer — a force multiplier whose presence turns “barely holding together” into “moving with purpose.”

The Ledger Index

9 tags × 10 numbers

Typhen doesn’t name people — he indexes them by the role they play in an outcome. A code name has two parts, and every combination of the two is already defined below.

Function Tag — the role
Vector pushes momentum forwardAnchor stabilizes, prevents driftRelay carries information between groupsPivot changes direction, reframesFuse binds elements togetherBreak disrupts or ends a patternSpan bridges gapsDraft pulls others alongQuiet subtle, decisive influence
Outcome Number — the behavior pattern, traced from Prime to Echo
01 initiates Prime·Initiation02 reinforces Prime·Initiation03 redirects Core·Stabilization04 escalates Core·Stabilization05 stabilizes Core·Stabilization06 dissolves Core·Stabilization07 synchronizes Core·Stabilization08 amplifies Core·Stabilization09 conceals Echo·Resolution10 reveals Echo·Resolution

Read straight down, 01 through 10 is one continuous arc: the same three phases as the Ledger Set’s marks, just under their other name. 01–02 are the opening move (Prime · Initiation), 03–08 are the sustained, in-motion behaviors (Core · Stabilization), 09–10 are what gets decided about the record afterward (Echo · Resolution).

FunctionTag-OutcomeNumber — e.g. Quiet-08, someone whose subtle influence amplifies others.
⚠ two systems, one vocabulary

Span, Break, Fuse, Relay, Anchor, and Quiet each do double duty: they’re code-name tags here, in the Ledger Index, and they’re also cut as physical marks in the Ledger Set below. Vector, Pivot, and Draft are index-only — they classify people, but Typhen carries no object they correspond to, so there’s no glyph for them.

The complete matrix — every tag, every number, made actionable

All 90 combinations, each as a self-contained field entry: the indexed asset, the function it draws from, where that outcome sits on the Prime-to-Echo arc, and what to actually do with it.
